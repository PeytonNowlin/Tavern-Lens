# Advisor decision datasets and recruit environments

Research date: 2026-10-02. Read-only web/source investigation; extends [September strategy research](advisor-strategy-and-data-research-2026-09-26.md) and [log/state research](logs-and-extractable-state.md). No provider outreach, purchases, external uploads, simulator installation, policy training, or application changes. Repository source inspection is evidence of interfaces and limitations, not a runtime fidelity certification.

## Main finding

The strongest immediately available decision source is our own archived `Power.log`, exported into **state before action → client options → action sent → confirmed effects → outcome**. We already retain much more evidence in compressed replays than our twelve-decision advisor traces expose. The searched public catalogs did not reveal a ready current-season, licensed Battlegrounds expert-action corpus. New simulation projects offer useful mechanics references, but their training data and evaluation require close inspection.

## A collector we can actually use now

### Evidence already saved

The coordinating agent's read-only marker count in replay seed `2095227427`, `Replays/Hearthstone_2026_10_02_16_37_51_L2_S2095227427.power.log.gz` under the app's Application Support directory, found **117 lines containing `SendOption`, 22 containing `SendChoices`, and 12,495 containing `DebugPrintOptions`**. These are line-marker counts, not unique or successfully executed player-action counts. The current client writes these namespaces into `Power.log`; a separate `GameState.log` is unnecessary. Raw private replay bytes are not included in this document or the repository.

[AdvisorDiagnostics.swift](../../Sources/TavernEngine/AdvisorDiagnostics.swift) currently stores `AdvisorDecision` as only `request` and `displayed`. Its trace retains the opening pair and at most eleven recent distinct pairs; repeated fingerprints replace the latest pair and excess intermediate pairs are discarded. The turn record has `capturedAt`, but the individual pairs do not. This is useful UI evidence, not a complete action trajectory.

Exact missing fields in those pairs: individual state/recommendation/display timestamps; outgoing option-group ID and selected option/suboption/target/position; observed player action and its provenance; the complete client options/errors/targets; confirmed post-action state/effects; adherence and reason for ignoring advice; and dropped/unlogged decision counts. Some of the *values* are recoverable from raw logs, but explicit advice display/adherence timestamps were never captured and cannot be invented retrospectively.

The current [PowerEvent](../../Sources/PowerParser/PowerEvent.swift) and [PowerLogParser](../../Sources/PowerParser/PowerLogParser.swift) expose offered `entityChoices` and server `entitiesChosen`; normal `Options`, outgoing `SendOption`, and outgoing `SendChoices` are not decoded. This is the precise prospective integration seam, beside existing choices—not an additional paid data feed.

### Independent parser and replay schema

Pinned [python-hslog parser](https://github.com/HearthSim/python-hslog/blob/015c0dec197779c90cd3131ecda2c990292342b6/hslog/parser.py#L638) handles client option groups, option entities/types/errors, targets/suboptions, `SendOption`, offered choices, outgoing choices and chosen entities. [Packet definitions](https://github.com/HearthSim/python-hslog/blob/015c0dec197779c90cd3131ecda2c990292342b6/hslog/packets.py#L184) preserve timestamps and the selected option/suboption/target/position. [HSReplay XML's DTD](https://github.com/HearthSim/hsreplay-xml/blob/master/hsreplay.dtd) can represent the same fields plus build, entity and state-change history. A replay format is not itself an accessible public replay corpus.

**Use a custom packet exporter.** The [default EntityTreeExporter](https://github.com/HearthSim/python-hslog/blob/015c0dec197779c90cd3131ecda2c990292342b6/README.md#L60) produces entity state and discards options/choices. A collector must join each outgoing selection to the preceding relevant option group, then snapshot state *before* effects. Keep invalid-option/error codes and choice IDs; only claiming all possible legal actions from final entities would lose target and interaction constraints.

Firestone's [XML parser package](https://github.com/Zero-to-Heroes/hs-replay-xml-parser/blob/9092c06bb84b81db1b4791d925dc471962e4db27/package.json) declares MIT and has Battlegrounds entity reconstruction. Its [post-match exporter](https://github.com/Zero-to-Heroes/hs-replay-xml-parser/blob/9092c06bb84b81db1b4791d925dc471962e4db27/src/lib/exrtactors/battlegrounds/battlegrounds-game-extractor.ts) exposes boards, health and per-turn reroll/freeze/power/buy/sell counts, rather than per-action shops. Its [purchase handler](https://github.com/Zero-to-Heroes/hs-replay-xml-parser/blob/9092c06bb84b81db1b4791d925dc471962e4db27/src/lib/exrtactors/battlegrounds/replay-parser.ts#L586) still does not retain which minion each purchase selected. Aggregate `BgsPostMatchStats` would repeat our data loss; inspect full packets/XML for labels.

### Proposed collection design

1. **Import one complete own replay offline first.** Preserve packet order, source namespace, timestamp and byte/line offset. Decode option and choice groups, outgoing selections, entity transitions and results. Report match-level completeness and a manually checked sample of buy, sell, play, roll, level, freeze, discover, hero power and Activate interactions. Preserve unsupported actions explicitly; never silently equate missing labels with “end turn.”
2. **Capture every meaningful live boundary.** At each outgoing player selection, retain the latest *visible* pre-action shop/hand/board, gold, tier/upgrade cost, health/armor, hero power, tribes, quests/rewards/trinkets/Dark Gifts, counters and option group. Save after-action effects as a separate record; future rolls/discover outcomes belong to subsequent states. Board reorders or other actions lacking an outgoing packet can receive confidence-marked transition inference, not an “observed click” label.
3. **Attach advice evidence and provenance.** Record request fingerprint, policy/version, data-source hashes and ages, request/recommendation/display times, pending/stale/unsupported state, candidate plans, and the selected actual action. Match only advice that was displayed before that action; unrelated advice is not adherence. Explicit human reasons remain optional annotations.
4. **Join outcomes and uncertainty.** Add next-combat result/damage, final placement, and available rank/MMR context. Keep unknown fields null with reasons. Use game/patch-level evaluation splits. Losing games, failed pivots and unsupported states must survive selection and retention.
5. **Start with an annotation pilot.** Review roughly 100 diverse real decision points: early economy/leveling, enabler commitments, pivots, low-health stabilization, discovers and missing mechanics. Label preferred short plans, acceptable alternatives, confidence and failure category. This is a proposed engineering pilot, not a statistically powered playing-strength experiment.

Important timing boundary: [the existing log research](logs-and-extractable-state.md#23-which-stream-to-parse-powertasklist-for-state-gamestate-for-metadata-and-choices) measured `GameState` combat information arriving ahead of on-screen animations; `PowerTaskList` supplies the screen-aligned state stream. An independent importer must track that distinction and choice/task-list phase boundaries. Do not let a future combat result, shop or hidden card leak into the pre-action observation simply because offline parsing can see the entire file. Our live engine's visible-state snapshot plus outgoing action is the clean prospective source.

## New recruit engine: useful mechanics, not a ready learned advisor

[HSBRSIM metadata](https://api.github.com/repos/Gallo13th/HSBRSIM/commits/4e0525a198352557ef816bd3a53566d6ffd4c164) pins September 1, 2026. Its README still names patch `35.6.0.243002`, but the newer `hsrl2` code contains BG36 mechanics; repository activity and README labels alone do not establish coverage.

- [Activate scripts](https://github.com/Gallo13th/HSBRSIM/blob/4e0525a198352557ef816bd3a53566d6ffd4c164/hsrl2/scripts/batches/batch_activate.py) register Living Prison and Lurking Lionfish, with [targeted tests](https://github.com/Gallo13th/HSBRSIM/blob/4e0525a198352557ef816bd3a53566d6ffd4c164/hsrl2/tests/test_batch_activate.py). These can seed questions and regression cases for our gaps. They also mark other Activate mechanics ambiguous/deferred; assertions against the author's own engine do not establish Hearthstone parity. Living Prison's multiple-instance pending-state semantics and Lionfish/Fishbait's complete buff/deathrattle chain deserve independent client-log verification.
- [Dark Gift selection](https://github.com/Gallo13th/HSBRSIM/blob/4e0525a198352557ef816bd3a53566d6ffd4c164/hsrl2/darkgifts.py) and [Season 14 tests](https://github.com/Gallo13th/HSBRSIM/blob/4e0525a198352557ef816bd3a53566d6ffd4c164/hsrl2/tests/test_s14_mechanics.py) contain 43 offer windows, eligibility filters and 36.2.1 constraints. The code explicitly treats rare-gift weighting and some tie rules as assumptions. These are rule references, not measured offer probabilities.
- The [RL full-game wrapper](https://github.com/Gallo13th/HSBRSIM/blob/4e0525a198352557ef816bd3a53566d6ffd4c164/hsrl/rl_env/envs/full_game_env.py) imports the older `hsrl` engine, defaults to skipping combat, and then assigns ranks by board score. It also auto-plays minions through list mutation and suppresses combat exceptions. The [action grammar](https://github.com/Gallo13th/HSBRSIM/blob/4e0525a198352557ef816bd3a53566d6ffd4c164/hsrl/rl_env/action/action_grammar.py) similarly targets that older engine and uses simplified action application. New card data does not upgrade this training path automatically.
- Its [training-status note](https://github.com/Gallo13th/HSBRSIM/blob/4e0525a198352557ef816bd3a53566d6ffd4c164/docs/AGENT_TRAINING_STATUS.md) reports heuristic imitation/board-score experiments and identifies real human trajectories as future work. This does not establish current-season placement improvement.

Recommended use: independently verify one missing mechanic against our real replay, then borrow reviewed test cases/interface ideas as permitted. Replacing our engine or training on its synthetic ranks would require a separate fidelity project.

## Dataset claims that did not hold up

**DeepBattler.** The [GitHub README](https://github.com/william-Dic/DeepBattler/blob/da31b8cfebab9ef30ce7af5cc57a19ef8dd9ef07/README.md) advertises expert-level RL advice and limits software reuse to noncommercial contexts. The [public Space RL listing](https://huggingface.co/api/spaces/iteratehack/deepbattler/tree/fed1ca78e8f0059401bc6336f1df83b49b8ab73e/RL?recursive=true) contains scripts but no advertised `RL/datasets` directory. [Shipped game history](https://github.com/william-Dic/DeepBattler/blob/da31b8cfebab9ef30ce7af5cc57a19ef8dd9ef07/Agent/game_history.json) has an empty action and null final rank; [collector code](https://github.com/william-Dic/DeepBattler/blob/da31b8cfebab9ef30ce7af5cc57a19ef8dd9ef07/DeepBattlerPlugin/Class1.cs) describes action labels as LLM suggestions. Its [GRPO reward](https://huggingface.co/spaces/iteratehack/deepbattler/blob/fed1ca78e8f0059401bc6336f1df83b49b8ab73e/RL/train_battleground_rlaif_gamehistory.py) and [evaluation](https://huggingface.co/spaces/iteratehack/deepbattler/blob/fed1ca78e8f0059401bc6336f1df83b49b8ab73e/RL/eval_battleground_rlaif_gamehistory.py) match candidate sequences rather than play games. [The adapter metadata](https://huggingface.co/api/models/iteratehack/battleground-rlaif-qwen-gamehistory-grpo) dates it to November 30, 2025, with no dataset/license information in its card metadata. No current trained-play performance was verified.

**Public dataset catalogs.** October 2 title searches in the [Hugging Face Hearthstone listing](https://huggingface.co/api/datasets?search=hearthstone&limit=100), [Battleground listing](https://huggingface.co/api/datasets?search=battleground&limit=100), and [Kaggle Hearthstone listing](https://www.kaggle.com/api/v1/datasets/list?search=hearthstone&pageSize=100) returned card text/code, translations, images, constructed decks, unrelated PUBG data and similar records—not a verified Battlegrounds shop/action/outcome corpus. For example, [dvitel/hearthstone](https://huggingface.co/datasets/dvitel/hearthstone) maps ordinary Hearthstone card descriptions to Python code. These are bounded catalog searches, not proof no private or differently named corpus exists.

**Older expert-video research.** The authors' [2022 MCTS paper](https://easychair.org/publications/paper/Kftf/download) extracted three Dogdog winning games from 2021 videos, using six early turns per dataset. Its experiment compares short-horizon simulated combat outcomes, not measured full-match ladder strength; no downloadable decision corpus or reuse grant was identified in the paper. It shows that careful VOD annotation is possible, not that those obsolete states should train today's policy.

**Other fresh repositories.** [The Local Tavern](https://github.com/uYgnaiJ/hearthstone-battleground-simulator) was pushed in September 2026 but intentionally simulates an Origins pool from build 35747/15.6 and documents fidelity gaps. [BobsDaddy](https://github.com/miaostudy/BobsDaddy) has a 2026 push but its README leaves data parsing, recruiting/combat simulation and RL environment work unchecked. [Neural-BGSimulator](https://github.com/LaunchGemini/Neural-BGSimulator) was last pushed in 2024, excludes heroes, and states all rights reserved. None is a current complete expert action dataset. The [Battlegrounds Match Data plugin](https://github.com/jawslouis/Battlegrounds-Match-Data) documents result/tier/combat-board exports, not full before-choice shops; adding another final-board collector does not address our missing action labels.

## Decision

Build the independent one-game action exporter first; expand local capture next. Review missing effects against `hsrl2` scripts and real-client evidence, then assemble a small expert-labeled decision set. Keep population statistics, expert rules, observed player actions and synthetic simulator labels explicitly separate. Final boards and unverified “expert-trained” model claims cannot substitute for decisions that contain the information available when the choice was made.
